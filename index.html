      Process

      More than just editing.

      I focus on the details that make short-form content hard to scroll past: pacing, story, visual hierarchy, sound, motion, and knowing the exact moment to cut.

      1. 01

        Hook

        The first second decides everything. I find the line, the look or the moment that earns the next three.

      2. 02

        Story

        Every clip gets a shape: a setup, a turn and a payoff, even in thirty seconds.

      3. 03

        Pacing

        Breaths, stumbles and dead air go. The energy stays up and you still sound like you.

      4. 04

        Visuals

        Captions, B-roll and motion graphics that guide the eye and support the message, never compete with it.

      5. 05

        Sound

        Music, effects and a clean vocal mix. People feel sound long before they notice it.

      6. 06

        Polish

        Color, framing, safe zones and exports for every platform. The details nobody points out and everybody feels.
